The two economies

The gap between Ireland's GDP and the real economy keeps widening

A handful of multinationals book vast profits through Ireland, inflating GDP far beyond what the domestic economy actually produces. The CSO built a bespoke measure — modified GNI* — to strip that distortion out. The distance between the two lines is, in effect, the multinational sector.

Society

A growing, changing population

Ireland is the fastest-growing country in the EU, and for the first time in modern history that growth is driven by migration rather than natural increase.

Housing

Prices at record highs, supply still short of need

The residential property price index has cleared its 2007 peak, even as completions have quadrupled from their post-crash low. Building more has not, so far, brought prices down.
